Request for Student Immunization Records

October 12, 2026

Dear Parents and Guardians,

To keep student records complete and accurate, Joy Academy Online asks each family to complete the Student Immunization Record Form and provide an official vaccination record.

The California Department of Public Health (CDPH) explains that California schools must maintain immunization records for all enrolled children. Under our Immunization Records Policy, Joy Academy Online collects this documentation as part of each student's permanent cumulative record.

Please complete the following steps:

  1. Complete the form: Enter student information, check the vaccines documented in the official record, and copy the vaccination dates. Leave unused date spaces blank; additional dates may remain in the attached official record. The number of date spaces does not indicate mandatory dose counts.
  2. Attach an official record: Provide a copy of an immunization record or equivalent vaccination history from a physician, clinic, hospital, school, government health authority, or other recognized medical source. International students may submit official records issued in their country or region of residence.
  3. Medical exemption, if applicable: Check the medical exemption box and attach supporting documentation from a licensed healthcare provider.
  4. Sign and date: Read the certification, enter the parent or guardian's name, sign by hand, and date the form. Leave the School Use Only section for school review.

This request is for record collection, not an instruction to receive every vaccine listed on the form. It does not assert that California classroom-based school-entry vaccination requirements apply to every overseas or exclusively online student. Consult a licensed healthcare professional about vaccination questions.

The Student Immunization Record Form is attached to this letter. Please complete the attached form. Print on US Letter at Actual Size, or on A4 using Fit to Paper. After completing and signing the form, submit it and the vaccination record documents to the school.

For more information, please visit the school website: https://joyacademy.online/immunization-requirement.html.

Joy Academy Online will maintain this documentation as part of the student's permanent cumulative record and may disclose information as required by applicable law. Please ensure submitted records are accurate and current.

Thank you for helping us maintain complete and accurate student records.

Sincerely,
Joy Academy Online

Official reference: CDPH — California School Immunization Law
