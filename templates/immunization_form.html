Immunization Records Policy

All enrolled students must provide an official immunization record or equivalent vaccination history from a physician, clinic, hospital, school, government health authority, or other recognized medical source. International students may submit official vaccination records issued in their country or region of residence.

Joy Academy Online maintains immunization records as part of each student's permanent cumulative record. Families are responsible for ensuring that submitted records are accurate and current. Students with medical exemptions must provide supporting documentation from a licensed healthcare provider.

疫苗接种记录政策

所有注册学生均须提交由医生、诊所、医院、学校、政府卫生机构或其他认可医疗机构出具的正式疫苗接种记录或疫苗接种历史证明。国际学生可提交其居住国家或地区认可机构签发的疫苗接种记录。

美嘉在线学堂将把疫苗接种记录作为学生永久学籍档案的一部分予以保存。家长有责任确保所提交记录真实、准确且为最新版本。如学生申请医疗豁免，须提交持牌医疗机构或医生出具的相关证明文件。
