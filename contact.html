Get in Touch

Whether you have questions about our programs, the admissions process, or anything else — our team is here to help.

Contact Protocol

For the most efficient resolution of questions and concerns, we recommend the following steps:

  1. Subject-related questions: Contact the subject teacher directly
  2. General questions: Contact your child's Homeroom Teacher
  3. Unresolved issues: Reach out to the administrative team

Admissions Inquiry

Interested in enrolling your child? Please email us at info@joyacademyonline.com with your child's name, current grade level, and any questions you have. We will follow up within 2 business days to schedule a welcome call.
