About Us

Seek Truth · Know Thyself · Serve Others

Welcome to Joy Academy Online

Dear Students, Families, and Educators,

Welcome to Joy Academy Online — a learning community where excellence is pursued with purpose and every voice is honored. We are founded on a simple but powerful belief: every student deserves access to transformative, high-quality education, regardless of where they live or what circumstances they face. Through our innovative online model, we provide the academic rigor, bilingual depth, and personal care that students need to grow, mature, and flourish in a globalized world.

At Joy Academy, three timeless principles shape everything we do: Seek Truth, Know Thyself, and Serve Others. We seek truth through rigorous, standards-aligned instruction that honors both global best practices and local educational traditions. We know ourselves by engaging deeply with our learning, reflecting honestly on our growth, and embracing the unique gifts each of us brings. We serve others by cultivating empathy, integrity, and a genuine commitment to the common good.

Our students learn in a dynamic bilingual environment that integrates English and Chinese, fostering both linguistic proficiency and cross-cultural understanding. Our virtual classrooms are designed to spark curiosity, challenge assumptions, and cultivate independent thinking — preparing students not only for international secondary education, but for lives of meaning and contribution.

As Principal, I am committed to building a school culture rooted in transparency, compassion, and authentic growth. Our teachers are empowered to teach with clarity and conviction. Our students are challenged to think critically and live purposefully. And our families are invited to walk alongside us in this sacred journey of education.

Whether you are new to Joy Academy or continuing your journey with us, I invite you to engage wholeheartedly. Together, let us pursue truth, discover who we truly are, and live lives of meaningful service.

With joy and anticipation,

Hongmei Su Principal, Joy Academy Online

Educational Philosophy

School Motto: Seek Truth · Know Thyself · Serve Others  |  求真 · 识己 · 达人

Seek Truth

"The truth will set you free." — John 8:32

Truth is discovered, not given. Through rigorous, curiosity-driven learning, we equip students to ask hard questions and follow where the evidence leads.

Know Thyself

"Who looks outside, dreams; who looks inside, awakes." — Carl Jung

Self-knowledge is the foundation of growth. We help students recognize the unique gifts God has placed within them — and grow into who they were made to be.

Serve Others

"Serve one another humbly in love." — Galatians 5:13

Service is love in action. We cultivate empathy, integrity, and a heart for others — preparing students to make a difference in the world around them.

At Joy Academy Online, teachers are not only educators but mentors — called to embody excellence, empathy, and faith in action. Families are cherished partners in this journey, walking alongside us as we help students grow into lives of purpose, joy, and service.

Vision

Joy Academy Online is committed to building a global online learning platform rooted in faith, anchored in international curriculum standards, and driven by technology — empowering students, wherever they are, to receive life-transforming education and live a meaningful, influential, and abundant life.

Mission

Our mission is to provide high-quality, bilingual education rooted in both international best practices and local standards. Through AP curriculum and teacher empowerment, we deliver transformative learning experiences that support holistic growth in academics, character, and faith. We equip students to seek truth, know themselves, and grow through serving others — preparing them to thrive as purposeful leaders in a globalized world.

Core Values — Joy LIFE

At Joy Academy Online, we believe values are not words on a wall — they are habits practiced daily, choices made under pressure, and commitments kept even when no one is watching. Our four core values — Love, Integrity, Faith, and Excellence — spell out Joy LIFE: the kind of life we want every student, teacher, and family to live. We do not claim to have mastered these values. But we try harder every day to embody them — in our classrooms, in our conversations, in how we handle difficulty, and in how we celebrate one another's growth.

Love

Caring for every person in our community with compassion and grace.

  • Seeking truth together requires the safety only love creates — we listen without judgment and speak without fear.
  • Love calls us to know ourselves honestly: our biases, our blind spots, and our capacity to grow.
  • We serve others not out of obligation but because love moves us — genuinely and without counting the cost.
  • We create a community where every person feels seen, valued, and worthy of belonging.
  • We believe care for one another is not separate from academic rigor — it is the foundation of it.

Integrity

Doing what is right — in words, in work, and in character.

  • We seek truth honestly — even when it challenges our assumptions or requires us to say "I was wrong."
  • We know ourselves clearly: acknowledging our strengths without pride, and our weaknesses without shame.
  • We serve others with transparency — our motives are open, our commitments are real, and our word is our bond.
  • We hold high academic standards and reject shortcuts, because integrity and excellence are inseparable.
  • We do what is right not because we are being watched, but because it reflects who we are.

Faith

Grounded in timeless principles that give meaning and direction.

  • Faith sends us in pursuit of truth deeper than data — meaning, purpose, and the value of a human life.
  • Faith grounds our sense of self: we know who we are because we trust in something greater than ourselves.
  • Faith moves us to serve others even when it is inconvenient, believing our efforts carry lasting meaning.
  • We approach all learning — scientific, historical, literary — with humility, wonder, and openness.
  • We hold hope as a discipline: trusting that every student can grow, every community can improve.

Excellence

Pursuing the highest standards in academics, character, and service.

  • We pursue truth rigorously — not satisfied with surface knowledge, but committed to deep, honest understanding.
  • We know ourselves well enough to see our own potential, and courageous enough to pursue it without settling.
  • We serve others with our best — because half-hearted effort serves no one well.
  • Excellence is not perfectionism; it is the daily habit of giving what we are capable of and growing that capacity.
  • We model excellence in everything — from how we teach to how we communicate, from lesson design to how we treat a struggling student.
Joy LIFE is not a checklist — it is a culture. When Love, Integrity, Faith, and Excellence are practiced together every day, they shape students who are not only academically prepared, but ready to lead lives of purpose, impact, and joy.

History

Joy Academy Online was officially founded in August 2025, based in Irvine, California. Registered in the state of California as a non-profit educational organization, Joy Academy Online exists for one purpose: to serve all students — regardless of background, location, or circumstance — with transformative, high-quality education.

The founding team brings over 16 years of international education experience across the United States and Asia, supporting multiple schools, designing curricula, and helping students transition successfully into international academic programs. That depth of experience — in classrooms, in school leadership, and in curriculum development — shapes every decision we make at Joy Academy Online.

As the global education landscape evolved and online learning demonstrated its power to reach students everywhere, we built a comprehensive, full-subject bilingual curriculum system. Joy Academy Online is the culmination of that journey: a school designed from the ground up to be excellent, accessible, and deeply human.

Statement of Nondiscrimination

Joy Academy Online is committed to providing a safe, respectful, and inclusive learning environment for all students, families, educators, and staff. We do not discriminate on the basis of race, color, national origin, ethnicity, language, gender, religion, disability, age, or socioeconomic status in the administration of our educational programs, admissions policies, or any other school-administered activities.
