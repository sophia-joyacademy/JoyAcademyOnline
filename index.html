Joy Academy Online

Seek Truth · Know Thyself · Serve Others

A global online international school for students in Grades 5–12

Joy Academy Online is a California-registered non-profit international school, founded in August 2025 and based in Irvine, California. We serve students in Grades 5–12 through a bilingual, AP-aligned online curriculum that nurtures the whole person — mind, heart, and spirit. Built by educators with over 16 years of international experience, we exist to make transformative, high-quality education accessible to all students, wherever they are.

School Motto: Seek Truth · Know Thyself · Serve Others  |  求真 · 识己 · 达人
