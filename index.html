Joy Academy Online

Seek Truth · Know Thyself · Serve Others

A global online international school for students in Grades 5–12

School Overview

School Motto: Seek Truth · Know Thyself · Serve Others  |  求真 · 识己 · 达人

Vision

Joy Academy Online is committed to building a global online learning platform rooted in faith, anchored in international curriculum standards, and driven by technology – empowering students, wherever they are, to receive life-transforming education and live a meaningful, influential, and abundant life.

Mission

At Joy Academy Online, our mission is to provide high-quality, bilingual education rooted in both international best practices and local standards. Through AP curriculum and teacher empowerment, we deliver transformative learning experiences that support holistic growth in academics, character, and faith. We equip students to seek truth, know themselves, and grow through serving others — preparing them to thrive as purposeful leaders in a globalized world.

Core Values

Joy Academy Online nurtures four core values in our community: love, integrity, faith, and excellence. These values guide our daily decisions, shape our culture, and define the kind of community we strive to build.

Compassion and care for every member of our community

⚖️

Trust in timeless principles that guide our lives

History and Purpose

Joy Academy Online began its educational journey in the United States, where it supported multiple schools and gained deep insight into classroom practices and instructional philosophy. The platform later expanded its services across Asia, assisting schools in implementing bilingual instruction and helping students transition smoothly into international curricula.

Our team has played a key role in the founding and management of an international K–12 school, focusing on curriculum design and instructional quality. In recent years, Joy Academy Online has built a comprehensive, full-subject online curriculum system serving students globally.

Educational Philosophy

At Joy Academy Online, we believe education is a sacred calling — one that nurtures the whole person: mind, heart, and spirit. Our philosophy is rooted in the conviction that every student is uniquely created by God, endowed with dignity, purpose, and boundless potential.

Our curriculum weaves together intellectual rigor and spiritual formation, guiding students to think deeply, act ethically, and lead with humility. We celebrate the diversity of our community while remaining anchored in values that transcend culture and circumstance: love, integrity, faith, and excellence.

We strive to cultivate a learning environment where:

  • Truth is pursued with courage — through rigorous inquiry, honest reflection, and alignment with both academic standards and timeless principles
  • Identity is discovered with intention — as students explore their gifts, wrestle with meaning, and grow in wisdom and character
  • Service is lived out with faithfulness — through acts of compassion, justice, and stewardship that reflect a commitment to the common good
