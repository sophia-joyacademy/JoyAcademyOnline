Academic Programs Overview

Joy Academy Online is an online school guided by truth, committed to excellence, and dedicated to honoring every student's voice. We offer a comprehensive, credit-bearing international curriculum for students in Grades 5–12, aligned with U.S. standards and designed to prepare graduates for admission to English-instruction universities worldwide.

The 5C School-Wide Learning Outcomes

At Joy Academy Online, we believe the most important thing we can give a student is not just knowledge — it is the ability to keep learning for the rest of their life. Our 5C framework defines the lifelong skills every JAO graduate carries into their future: into universities, workplaces, communities, and families. These are not abstract ideals. They are practiced every day, in every class, at every grade level.

Communication

Expressing and listening with clarity, honesty, and sensitivity across cultures and contexts.

  • We value honest, clear, and direct interaction.
  • We listen actively so that we can deeply understand others.
  • We use and pay attention to both verbal and non-verbal skills.
  • We are sensitive to different styles of communication.
  • We seek to make our conversations efficient and productive.

Collaboration

Respecting differences and taking responsibility for one another in every community we belong to.

  • We respect and appreciate people who are different from us.
  • We take responsibility for one another in our attitudes and actions.
  • We follow rules which allow us to live in harmony with one another.
  • We provide honest and constructive feedback to one another.
  • We value the different strengths that each individual brings.

Critical Thinking

Looking at everything from multiple angles — with depth, rigor, and intellectual honesty.

  • We look at everything from multiple angles and perspectives.
  • We avoid oversimplified answers to complex problems.
  • We use strategies to come to well-reasoned conclusions.
  • We approach problems systematically.
  • We apply analytic and scientific methods appropriately.

Creativity

Approaching every challenge with curious eyes, an open mind, and the courage to explore.

  • We approach problems with curious and fresh eyes.
  • We have the openness and courage to explore the unknown.
  • We don't always take the old path to get to our goal.
  • We expect to find truth in unexpected and surprising places.
  • We celebrate the generation of new ideas and solutions.

Confidence

Acting on our beliefs and values — embracing difficulty, leadership, and growth without fear.

  • We are secure in our beliefs and values and act accordingly.
  • We are not diminished by the fear of failure.
  • We do not shy away from pain or difficulty.
  • We take the initiative and embrace leadership opportunities.
  • We understand that all improvement is a never-ending process.
These outcomes reflect our unwavering commitment to cultivating well-rounded, future-ready learners who can think critically, lead with purpose, and thrive in fast-changing, complex global environments. The 5Cs are not a checklist — they are a way of living and learning that we practice together, every day, at every level of school.

Students may also earn elective credits through independent study (120 documented hours = 1 credit, up to 1 per year), subject to department approval.

Grading & Assessment

Grades at Joy Academy Online reflect more than content mastery. Every course blends measurable knowledge with our 5C school-wide learning outcomes and is built around increasing levels of cognitive rigor, guided by Bloom's Taxonomy. Learning is assessed through four types of work — formative checks, summative tests, performance tasks, and participation — and each quarter's grade is calculated from six weighted categories: Classroom Contribution and Language Skills (10% each), Homework and Quiz (15% each), and Unit Test and Midterm/Final Exam (25% each). Coursework is designed so students move beyond recall, building from foundational thinking (Remember, Understand) toward higher-order skills (Apply, Analyze, Evaluate, Create).

Differentiated Instruction

We believe every student deserves teaching at their level. English and Social Studies classes are grouped by English proficiency level, while Math and Science classes are grouped by academic level. For example, a student may begin with Pre-Algebra in Grade 6 and advance to AP Calculus by Grade 11 or 12, following a sequence tailored to their pace. This flexibility ensures no student is left behind and no student is held back.

Foreign Language is set at 2 credits for both tracks: as native Chinese speakers immersed in a bilingual curriculum, JAO students already demonstrate strong language proficiency — those additional credits are better invested in AP or advanced elective coursework.

Age Requirement

All students must graduate by May 31st of their graduation year, prior to their 20th birthday.

Full-Time Enrollment Requirement

To be eligible for the Joy Academy Online diploma, students must be enrolled full-time (a minimum of 7 courses per year) during Grades 11 and 12. This ensures that every diploma graduate has been fully supported through the most critical years of college preparation.

Students in Grades 5–10 may enroll part-time, taking individual courses at their own pace. Credits earned part-time count toward diploma requirements if the student later transitions to full-time enrollment.

Two Enrollment Tracks

  • Full-Time (Diploma Track) — Minimum 7 courses per year (English, Mathematics, Science, Social Studies, College & Career Research Seminar, and 2 electives). Required in Grades 11–12. Includes full access to college counseling, academic advising, and transcript support.
  • Part-Time (Individual Courses) — Students may take 1–6 courses per year. Credits are recorded on official transcripts but do not lead to the JAO diploma unless the student transitions to full-time in Grades 11–12.

Students who complete Grades 10–12 at Joy Academy Online and fulfill all graduation requirements will also earn the Rockford Christian School High School Diploma, an accredited U.S. high school credential recognized by universities worldwide.

GPA and Academic Recognition

Joy Academy Online uses a standard 4.0 GPA scale: A = 4.0, A− = 3.7, B+ = 3.3, B = 3.0, B− = 2.7, C+ = 2.3, C = 2.0, and so on. AP and Honor courses carry additional weight, rewarding students who challenge themselves. Strong academic performance is recognized each semester.

College and University Preparation

Our dedicated college counselors begin working with students in Grade 11, providing personalized guidance through every stage: standardized test planning (TOEFL, IELTS, SAT), university selection, application essays, recommendation letter strategy, and scholarship applications.

Our goal is for every Joy Academy graduate to gain admission to an English-instruction university — including institutions in the QS World University Rankings Top 200.
