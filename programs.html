Programs

Grades 5–12 · Bilingual · AP Curriculum · Credit-Bearing

Academic Programs Overview

Joy Academy Online is an online school guided by truth, committed to excellence, and dedicated to honoring every student's voice. We offer a comprehensive, credit-bearing international curriculum for students in Grades 5–12, aligned with U.S. standards and designed to prepare graduates for admission to English-instruction universities worldwide.

The 5C Teaching Approach

Our bilingual (English + Chinese) immersive environment develops five core competencies essential in the modern world:

C

Communication

Expressing ideas clearly and persuasively in both English and Chinese

C

Collaboration

Working effectively with peers across cultures and disciplines

C

Critical Thinking

Analyzing, questioning, and evaluating information with depth and rigor

C

Creativity

Exploring new ideas, producing original work, approaching challenges with imagination

C

Confidence

Building self-assurance and resilience to take on academic and life challenges

Course Offerings (Grades 5–12)

English

ESL 1, ESL 2, English 1, English 2, English 3, English 4, Honor English 1, Honor English 2, AP Language Arts, AP Literature

Social Studies

Supplemental English, The Ancient World, The Medieval World, U.S. Through Industrialism, World History (Regular & Honor), European History (Regular, Honor, AP), U.S. History (Regular, Honor, AP), U.S. Government, AP Macroeconomics, AP Microeconomics

Mathematics

Grade 5 Math, Pre-Algebra, Intro Algebra, Algebra 1, Geometry, Algebra 2, AP Pre-Calculus, AP Calculus AB/BC, AP Statistics, Linear Algebra

Science

Earth Science, Life Science, Geography, HS Biology, Chemistry, Physics, AP Physics I & II

Chinese Language Arts

Middle School Chinese Reading & Writing I–II; High School Chinese Reading & Writing I–II

Project-Based Learning (PBL)

Middle School PBL, High School PBL — students tackle real-world challenges through interdisciplinary projects that build teamwork and innovation skills.

Computer Studies

MS Office (Middle School); Computer Science, AP Computer Science (High School)

College Planning

College Planning & Application I (Grade 11), College Planning & Application II (Grade 12)

Students may also earn elective credits through independent study (120 documented hours = 1 credit, up to 1 per year), subject to department approval.

Differentiated Instruction

We believe every student deserves teaching at their level. English and Social Studies classes are grouped by English proficiency level, while Math and Science classes are grouped by academic level. For example, a student may begin with Pre-Algebra in Grade 6 and advance to AP Calculus by Grade 11 or 12, following a sequence tailored to their pace. This flexibility ensures no student is left behind and no student is held back.

Graduation Requirements

To earn the Joy Academy Online diploma, students must complete 28 total credits:

  • English — 4 credits
  • Chinese Language Arts — 4 credits
  • Mathematics — 4 credits
  • Science — 4 credits
  • Social Science — 4 credits
  • Project-Based Learning — 4 credits
  • Computer Studies — 1–2 credits
  • Electives — 2–3 credits

Students must be enrolled full-time and complete their senior year with us.

Students who complete three consecutive years (Grades 10–12) and fulfill all graduation requirements will also earn the Rockford Christian School High School Diploma, an accredited U.S. high school credential recognized by universities worldwide.

GPA and Academic Recognition

Joy Academy Online uses a standard 4.0 GPA scale: A = 4.0, A− = 3.7, B+ = 3.3, B = 3.0, B− = 2.7, C+ = 2.3, C = 2.0, and so on. AP and Honor courses carry additional weight, rewarding students who challenge themselves. Strong academic performance is recognized each semester.

College and University Preparation

Our dedicated college counselors begin working with students in Grade 11, providing personalized guidance through every stage: standardized test planning (TOEFL, IELTS, SAT), university selection, application essays, recommendation letter strategy, and scholarship applications.

Our goal is for every Joy Academy graduate to gain admission to an English-instruction university — including institutions in the QS World University Rankings Top 200.
