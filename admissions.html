Admissions

Serving students worldwide in Grades 5–12

Who We Serve

Joy Academy Online is ideal for families who:

  • Value both character formation and academic achievement
  • Want their child to have a pathway to international higher education
  • Are committed to investing in long-term, systematic learning

We serve students in Grades 5–12 from anywhere in the world.

Age Eligibility

To be eligible for enrollment, students must be able to graduate by May 31st of their graduation year, prior to their 20th birthday. Age eligibility is reviewed at the time of application and confirmed at each annual enrollment renewal.

Why Choose Joy Academy Online

We understand the real challenges families face when pursuing international education. Here is how Joy Academy Online meets them:

Curriculum

Complete, Structured Curriculum

Systematic teaching materials with clear semester goals and standardized test timelines, supported by experienced college counselors.

Faculty

Highly Qualified Teachers

Over 50% hold master's or doctoral degrees; all teachers are vetted for subject expertise and teaching excellence.

Support

Bilingual Homeroom Teachers

Every student has a dedicated homeroom teacher who monitors daily progress and provides parents with written updates each week.

Community

Peer Learning Community

Structured co-learning groups help students build discipline, accountability, and lasting friendships.

Learning

PBL and Seasonal Camps

Project-Based Learning develops teamwork and innovation; themed camps provide seasonal enrichment.

University

Personalized College Advising

Tailored university pathways with scholarship support make world-class higher education accessible for every family.

Our Faculty

  • English Language Arts and Social Studies taught by native English-speaking teachers
  • Math, Science, and ESL taught by highly qualified bilingual instructors (over 50% hold Master's or Doctoral degrees)
  • Each student is assigned a bilingual homeroom teacher who monitors daily learning and communicates weekly with parents
  • Teaching assistants ensure every student keeps pace with the curriculum
  • Dedicated university counselors help high school students plan individualized college application pathways

Application Process

  1. Submit Application — Complete the online application form, providing student information and parent contact details.
  2. Admissions Interview — We schedule a welcome call to learn about the student's learning background, language level, and family needs.
  3. Level Placement — Based on the interview and prior academic records, we confirm grade level, course schedule, and start date.
  4. Official Enrollment — Sign the enrollment agreement and receive student account, course schedule, and orientation materials.

We respond within 2 business days of receiving your application.
